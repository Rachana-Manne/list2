My Top Travel Destinations

Lists help organize information so it's easy to scan and understand. Below I've used an ordered list to rank my dream trips (the order matters!) and an unordered list for the essentials I'd pack on any of them (the order doesn't). Each list also demonstrates a different list-style attribute.

Top 5 Dream Destinations

An ordered list using Roman numerals (type="I").

  1. Kyoto, Japan — temples, tea houses, and cherry blossoms.
  2. Reykjavík, Iceland — northern lights and geothermal springs.
  3. Marrakech, Morocco — vibrant souks and historic medinas.
  4. Patagonia, Argentina — glaciers and dramatic mountain trails.
  5. Lisbon, Portugal — pastel architecture and coastal cuisine.
